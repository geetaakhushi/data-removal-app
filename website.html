How it works

A simple, review-first workflow.

1
Find a listing Add a listing document and record which website displays your information.
2
Prepare a request Draft a polite request asking the organisation to review and remove the listed data.
3
Review and follow up Check every detail yourself before sending; track the response manually.

Important to know

This HTML prototype does not scan the selected file, send email, place calls, or delete data from third-party websites. It stores demo records in your browser only.

Do not enter real sensitive information into a demo or shared computer. Verify the applicable law and the organisation’s process before sending a request.
